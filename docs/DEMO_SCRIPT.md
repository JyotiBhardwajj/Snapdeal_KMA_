# Screen-recording script (1–2 minutes)

**Setup**
- Use Chrome on a laptop and wear **headphones** (laptop speakers also work, thanks to the echo guard).
- Open the demo URL and set the window to about 1440×900. Start a recorder that captures **both your mic and the
  tab audio** (Loom, OBS, or macOS QuickTime + BlackHole).
- Allow microphone access. Do one warm-up call off camera so the service is warm.

**Main take (about 2 minutes)**

| Time | You do / say | What to point at |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | *"This is Disha, a voice-first AI account manager for Snapdeal sellers. On the left is the call; on the right is a case file she fills in live."* Click **Call Disha**. | Account snapshot from the brief |
| 0:08 | Disha greets you. **Say:** *"Hi Disha, my sales have dropped a lot this month and I don't understand why."* | Status: "Analysing your sales data…" |
| 0:18 | Disha gives the headline numbers and asks what caused the stock-outs. | **Diagnosis:** sales bridge, verdicts, pricing ruled out, early warnings |
| 0:30 | **Say:** *"My Delhi supplier was three weeks late because of the festive rush."* | "What the seller told Disha" fills in |
| 0:40 | Disha asks a second question. **Say:** *"I update stock manually once a week, so I had to cancel some orders."* | |
| 0:52 | Disha presents the plan with real numbers (units, cost, sales brought back). *(Optional: interrupt her once to show barge-in.)* | **Action plan** + **Restock order** table |
| 1:10 | She mentions the coffee mugs running out. **Say:** *"Yes, set up the alerts and send me the list on WhatsApp."* | **Done on the seller's behalf** with reference IDs |
| 1:25 | **Say:** *"Yes, a follow-up next week is good. That's all, thanks!"* | Follow-up booked |
| 1:35 | Disha summarises, says goodbye and **hangs up on her own**. | **Call summary** card → Download |

**Second take, if you have 30 more seconds (shows ownership):** click **"Fast-forward 7 days"**. Disha calls back,
leads with the wins (sales +24%, cancellations down to 5%), chases the spice rack, warns that the coffee mugs are
almost out and offers the sale nomination. Point at **Progress since the last call**.

**Optional add-ons:** switch to **हिन्दी** (*"Mere sales kam kyun ho gaye?"*), or ask *"Should I just cut my prices?"*
to show her pushing back with data.

**Tips**
- Speak naturally and pause for about a second when you finish; Disha replies in roughly two seconds.
- If she mishears something, just correct her ("No, I meant…"). Handling that is part of the demo.
- Each call starts a fresh case file. Use **Call again** if the network drops.
